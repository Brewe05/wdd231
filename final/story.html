How we got here

Our Story

Seven years, two countries, a snowman in Clarence, and one very important question asked on 11 July.

1. The most amazing girl

I was two months after my two-year mission when I met her. She walked in, said something clever, and completely rearranged my priorities. I have never met anyone like Rebecca — the most amazing girl I have ever known.

2. Two trips, then a moving truck

The day my mission ended I started planning. I flew up twice just to spend time with her — long weekends, terrible road-trip playlists, and the growing certainty that I did not want to drive home again.

Two months later I packed everything I owned and moved across the country to be with her. Best decision I have ever made.

3. Adventures, big and small

Since then we have collected a ridiculous number of memories. There was the trip to Clarence where it snowed properly for the first time, and we built a snowman with a carrot we had to borrow from a very confused guesthouse owner.

There was the quad-biking safari where Rebecca out-rode me on every single dune. There were the little date nights at restaurants far too nice for our budget, and the live plays where we whispered predictions about the ending and were almost always wrong.

And there was the cave adventure — headlamps, tight passages, and one moment of complete darkness where she laughed instead of panicking. That is when I knew.

4. 11 July — she said yes

On 11 July I asked Rebecca to marry me. I had rehearsed it about forty times and forgot every word the moment she looked at me. It did not matter. She said yes.

We are now planning our wedding at Geko Ridge Wedding Venue near Pretoria, surrounded by green hills and everyone we love.

5. What comes next

On 21 October 2027 we will stand in front of our families and friends at Geko Ridge and promise each other the rest of our lives.

We would love nothing more than to have you there with us. Please send us your RSVP and find your seat on the details page.

Brent & Rebecca Welsford · 21 October 2027 · Geko Ridge, Pretoria
